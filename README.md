**MessMate – Smart Hostel Mess Management System**

MessMate is a centralized digital platform designed to address real-life challenges in hostel mess management at **NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT)**. Traditional mess operations often involve manual subscription management, meal tracking, payment calculations, add-on coordination, and food preparation estimates, leading to administrative overhead, limited transparency, inaccurate records, and food wastage.

MessMate brings these processes together in one platform. Students can choose between North Indian and South Indian mess plans and select additional food items such as eggs, milk, chicken, and other available add-ons for each upcoming month. A single permanent QR code enables convenient meal and add-on redemption, while preventing duplicate claims and maintaining accurate digital records.

The platform also provides flexible advance payments, transparent financial ledgers, payment tracking, and final settlement calculations. Administrators can manage student subscriptions, pricing, staff, meal schedules, attendance, food preparation, and operational reports through a unified dashboard. By using historical attendance and consumption data to estimate food requirements, MessMate also aims to reduce over-preparation and minimize food wastage.

**By addressing practical mess management problems faced in a real campus environment, MessMate aims to improve operational efficiency, financial transparency, accountability, and the overall dining experience at NMAMIT.**
